# Rocket Meter 0.99.0

- New: three themes for the meter window, in Configure > Appearance > Theme. **Blizzard Midnight** (the game's own meter strip, as before), **Blizzard Forever** (the title strip of the game's quest tracker, with its own font) and **Flare** (a dark translucent panel with a thin bronze frame, the title and buttons in bronze).
- New look for every other window (Configure, scoreboard, My run, the other player's screen, spells and statistics): a seal and title band, tabs on top and the Rocket family's buttons, dropdowns, checkboxes and sliders.
- Confirmation boxes (reset, report a problem) now open in the family's own window instead of the game's old dialog.
- Fixed: an open dropdown no longer lets the controls under it show through, and a button's or tab's text no longer disappears under the mouse.
