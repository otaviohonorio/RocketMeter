# Rocket Meter 0.93.0

- "My run" is now laid out like a Warcraft Logs report, in a wider window: Summary, Damage done, Damage taken, Healing, Deaths, Interrupts, Dispels, Casts and History.
- Summary: a chart of damage done, damage taken and healing per fight with your deaths, your attributes and your gear (item level, gems and enchants), and the top abilities in four boxes.
- Every fight keeps what each spell did, so a boss or a saved run opens with its own spell lists.
- A session that started by itself (open world, delve, dungeon) is saved when you log out or /reload.
- Inside dungeons, raids and scenarios the game's combat log is turned on for you and off when you leave (option "Record the combat log in instances", on by default). Only the log the addon turned on is touched.
- Casts count only what you pressed, inside fights; opening chests and unlocking are not casts.
- Scoreboard: sort by any column; shows the raid difficulty; the last boss of a key shows the numbers of its own fight; loot and score go only to the run that just ended; /rm score opens the most recent one.
- New option: square or round spec icon.
- Fixed: the "Support / Report a problem" box no longer breaks the game's DELETE box when destroying an item.
- Fixed: after a /reload in a key, earlier deaths no longer pile up; group inspection waits for the Inspect window to close.
- Switching or resetting a profile applies everything at once; tooltips follow the game's style; secondary text is easier to read.
