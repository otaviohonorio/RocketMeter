# Rocket Meter 0.92.0

- **"My run" works everywhere, not only in a key or a raid.** Click your own row in a delve, a
  normal or heroic dungeon, a scenario, a battleground or the open world and the same screen
  opens: what you did fight by fight, your casts, your cooldowns, the damage you took, your
  deaths. It starts by itself at your first fight and is named by where you are (the delve
  with its tier, the open world with its zone).
- **"Casts" and "Spells" are one tab now.** Each spell on one line: how many times you cast it,
  casts per minute, what it gave, per second and its share. What you cast that gave no damage
  or healing of its own is listed under it. The tab is built with the same pieces as the rest
  of the window and follows the fight and run filters.
- **Your talents in the Spells tab.** Two new sections on your own row: of your damage and of
  your healing, how much came from spells that a talent of your build teaches. A talent that
  only makes another spell stronger cannot be measured and is not listed.
- **Hover a row to see what that player cast.** The game's tooltip opens beside the row with the
  player's top spells, each with its amount and its share: damage first, then healing. The click
  still opens the full screen.
- Fixed: the Spells tab did not scroll when it had more than fitted in the window (the bar moved
  and the page stayed).
