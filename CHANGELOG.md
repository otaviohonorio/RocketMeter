# Rocket Meter 0.95.0

- **Now in 11 languages**: Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Italian, German, French, Russian, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), besides English and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows your game's language. Game terms are the game's own words in each language. The new translations were not reviewed by native speakers yet: corrections are welcome in the comments.
- The end-of-key scoreboard, the spell breakdown, the settings, My run and the teammate window now use the game's dark background and its own frames. The meter window itself is unchanged.
- The item you won on the Mythic+ end screen shows the game's own tooltip, with its bonuses and item level; Shift-click and Ctrl-click work as in the game.
- Brazilian Portuguese texts reviewed.
