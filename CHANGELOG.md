# Rocket Meter 0.94.0

- My run, Deaths: each death now shows the defensives you had ready and did not press (or that
  none was ready, all of them already used). The list of defensives comes from the game's tables.
- My run, Casts: for every cooldown of 45 s or more, how many times it was ready in those fights
  and the share of them you pressed it. Only a damage dealer's burst is coloured; defensives and
  what is pressed on demand are not judged.
