# Rocket Meter 0.91.0

- **The scoreboard button shows your last key when there is nothing measured.** With the meter
  empty, or right after clearing the data, the magnifier on the meter's window used to open an
  empty board. It now opens the last Mythic+ key you finished, as its final scoreboard.
- **Click your own row on the scoreboard** to open "My run" for that key: what you did in it
  and what it cost. On the board of the current session, your row opens the run in progress.
